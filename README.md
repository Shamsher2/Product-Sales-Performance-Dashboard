# 📊 Product Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Product Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze overall sales performance across **products, categories, regions, and monthly trends**.

The dashboard helps identify top-performing products, high-revenue categories, regional contribution, monthly sales trends, and profitability. It provides a consolidated view of key business metrics to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall revenue and profit performance.
* Identify the top-performing products.
* Compare revenue and profit across categories.
* Understand regional sales contribution.
* Analyze monthly revenue trends.
* Monitor total quantity sold and average revenue.
* Evaluate overall profit margin.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* **Electronics** generated the highest revenue with approximately **57M**.
* **Home & Furniture** generated the highest profit at approximately **11.2M**.
* The **East Region** contributed the highest revenue share at **31.59%**.
* Revenue increased significantly during **October and November**.
* **November** recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately **27M**.
* **Home & Furniture** was the strongest category in terms of profit.
* The dashboard shows clear differences in performance across regions, categories, and products.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

Provides a quick overview of:

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Total Quantity
* Average Revenue
* Profit Margin

### Regional Analysis

A donut chart shows the percentage contribution of:

* East
* West
* Centre
* South

### Product Analysis

Highlights the **Top 5 Products by Revenue**.

### Category Revenue Analysis

Compares revenue generated by different product categories.

### Monthly Revenue Trend

Shows how revenue changes throughout the year and identifies high-performing months.

### Category Profit Analysis

Compares profit generated across major product categories.

### Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard by **Region** to dynamically analyze performance.
